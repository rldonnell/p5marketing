## Spam-Avoidance Notes

- No image-heavy layout assumed — this is a plain-text-friendly structure
- No deceptive subject lines
- Personalization tokens ({first_name}, {sender_name}) signal legitimate sending
- Short, scannable paragraphs for mobile readability
- CTA uses descriptive anchor text, not generic "click here"
