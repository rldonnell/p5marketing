# Four Winds CMMS — Audience Lab Filter Recommendations

**Goal:** Build an audience of people actively researching facilities management / CMMS software who actually work in maintenance and operations roles.

**Problem with current audience:** Intent filters are catching the right companies, but without job title filtering, Audience Lab defaults to returning owners, office managers, and other generic contacts — not the maintenance professionals who'd evaluate and champion a CMMS.

---

## 1. Intent Filters

Use specific, high-intent keywords that signal someone is actively evaluating CMMS or maintenance management software. Avoid broad terms like "facilities management" alone, which can match janitorial, space planning, or real estate audiences.

**Recommended intent keywords (pick 2–4):**

- CMMS software
- computerized maintenance management system
- preventive maintenance software
- work order management software
- maintenance management software
- facility maintenance software
- asset management software maintenance

**Avoid or use cautiously:**

- "facilities management" alone (too broad — matches FM consulting, janitorial, space planning)
- "EAM software" (overlaps but skews toward enterprise buyers, may dilute the list)
- "building management" (pulls in BMS/BAS audiences, not maintenance)

---

## 2. Job Title Filters (this is the biggest fix)

This is what's missing from the current audience. Add a job title inclusion filter targeting maintenance and operations roles. These are the people who evaluate, recommend, and use CMMS daily.

**Primary titles (highest value):**

- Maintenance Manager
- Maintenance Director
- Facilities Manager
- Facilities Director
- Plant Manager
- Maintenance Supervisor
- Maintenance Planner
- Reliability Manager
- Reliability Engineer

**Secondary titles (still strong, broader reach):**

- VP of Operations
- Director of Operations
- Operations Manager
- Plant Engineer
- Facilities Coordinator
- Maintenance Technician Lead
- Chief Engineer
- Building Engineer
- Facilities Engineer

**Titles to EXCLUDE (these are polluting your current list):**

- Owner (unless paired with a maintenance/industrial company)
- Office Manager
- Marketing Manager
- Sales Manager
- Accounting Manager
- Project Coordinator (generic)
- National Sales Manager
- Graphics & Marketing Manager

---

## 3. Business / Industry Filters

Narrow to industries where CMMS adoption is strongest and where maintenance teams have real budget authority.

**Recommended industries:**

- Manufacturing (all sub-segments)
- Property Management / Commercial Real Estate
- Healthcare / Hospitals / Senior Living
- Education (universities, school districts)
- Government / Municipal
- Food & Beverage Processing
- Warehousing & Distribution
- Energy / Utilities
- Hospitality (hotels, resorts with large physical plants)

**Avoid or deprioritize:**

- Software / SaaS (these are competitors, not buyers)
- Marketing / Advertising
- Financial Services
- Legal
- Retail (unless large-footprint retail with maintenance teams)

---

## 4. Company Size Filter

CMMS buying behavior varies by company size. Smaller companies (under 20 employees) are less likely to need a full CMMS — they're the "Owner" contacts flooding your list.

**Recommended:** 50+ employees (sweet spot: 100–5,000)

This filters out sole proprietors and tiny shops where the owner is the only contact, which is why you're seeing so many "Owner" titles.

---

## 5. Contact Filters

If Audience Lab allows contact-level filtering beyond job title:

- **Seniority:** Manager level and above
- **Department:** Operations, Maintenance, Facilities, Engineering
- **Exclude:** Sales, Marketing, Finance, HR, Legal departments

---

| Filter | Current (Problem) | Recommended (Fix) |

| Intent | Likely broad ("facilities management software") | Specific ("CMMS software", "preventive maintenance software", "work order management software") |
| Job Title | No filter (returns default contacts) | Maintenance Manager, Facilities Director, Plant Manager, etc. |

| Company Size | No filter (includes 1-person shops) | 50+ employees |
| Department | No filter | Operations, Maintenance, Facilities |

---

## Quick-Start: Minimum Viable Filter Set

If Audience Lab limits how many filters you can stack, prioritize these three changes:

1. **Add job title filter** — include Maintenance Manager, Facilities Manager, Plant Manager, Maintenance Director, Facilities Director, Operations Manager
2. **Tighten intent keywords** — use "CMMS software" and "preventive maintenance software" instead of broad terms
3. **Set company size to 50+** — eliminates the flood of generic Owner contacts
