How VisitorID™ Works

A fully automated, four-stage pipeline that runs every night. No manual work required once you're onboarded.

01

Identify

A lightweight tracking pixel is installed on your website. When visitors browse your site, VisitorID™ resolves their identity — name, email, phone, mailing address, and demographic data — without requiring a form fill.

02

Classify

Every page a visitor views is mapped to the specific services, procedures, or products they're researching. You don't just learn who visited — you learn exactly what they're interested in.

03

Score

Our proprietary dual-scoring engine rates each visitor on both purchase intent and identity confidence. Hot leads are separated from casual browsers, and bots and fake records are filtered out automatically.

04

Deliver

Qualified leads arrive in your CRM every morning — tagged with their intent tier, specific interests, and traffic source. Your sales team starts the day with a prioritized list of people who are actively researching your services.
